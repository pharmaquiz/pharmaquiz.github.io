Capsules are virtual educational credits that may be used only within PharmaQuiz.

  • Capsules have no cash value.
  • Capsules cannot be exchanged for money.
  • Capsules cannot be transferred between users unless officially supported.
  • Capsules may be obtained through purchases, rewards, or promotions.
  • Capsules remain part of the PharmaQuiz service.

Improperly obtained Capsules may be removed where permitted by law.
