Delete Your Pharma Quiz Account

If you wish to delete your Pharma Quiz account and associated data, follow the steps below.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Pharma Quiz app.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your request.

What data will be deleted

  • User profile
  • Quiz progress
  • Bookmarks
  • Capsule balance
  • Account information associated with the app

Data retention

Your account and associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request unless required by law to retain certain information.
